Dear students,


The Term Project Proposal submission is now open on Ninova.

 

What to submit: Download the proposal template from Ninova, fill it in completely, and upload your completed form. Only one team member must submit of the proposal.

 

Important reminders:

Check the shared dataset list on Ninova before submitting — no two groups may use the same dataset.

Revision requests will be sent by the end of Week 6. If revisions are requested, you must re-submit. Failure to do so results in a 10% deduction from your project grade.
Use the proposal template available on Ninova. Do not use a different format.

Good luck!
